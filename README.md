# MiningOS — Pakistan's 1st Deep-Tech Exploration & Mining ERP Platform

An end-to-end Mining Intelligence and Operations platform designed for Pakistan's critical minerals (Copper, Chromite, Gold, Barite, Coal) in **Balochistan (Chagai / Reko Diq / Muslim Bagh)** and **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. High-Converting Landing Page
* **National Problem & Market Context:** Aligned with SIFC (Special Investment Facilitation Council) and Pakistan's $6+ Trillion mineral wealth.
* **The 4 Deep-Tech Pillars:**
  1. **Satellite AI Exploration:** Ingestion of European Space Agency (ESA Sentinel-2) & NASA ASTER Shortwave Infrared (SWIR) bands to identify Copper-Gold alteration zones without drilling CapEx.
  2. **Smart Weighbridge & Digital Bilti:** Direct RS232 scale serial capture, QR-coded digital challans, and auto provincial royalty tax calculation.
  3. **Fleet & Fuel Theft AI:** Telemetry correlating machine engine hours with diesel burn rates to alert on siphon theft in real time.
  4. **Ore Blending & Quality Control:** Precision blending algorithms to hit export contract grades (e.g. >42% Cr2O3 / Cu) without wasting high-grade ore.
* **Scientific Research Citations:** Citing USGS Chagai Belt and MDPI Balochistan Ophiolite remote sensing studies.
* **Executive Summary (One-Pager Modal):** Ready-to-show pitch deck for event delegates, mining leaseholders, and investors.

### 2. Live Interactive Dashboard Demo
* **Interactive Leaflet Satellite Map:** Centered on Balochistan (Chagai / Reko Diq: 28.98° N, 64.42° E) with toggleable Hyperspectral Mineral Heatmap layers and active lease geofences.
* **Real-time Weighbridge Stream:** Working simulator with gross/tare/net tonnage, purity assay %, and verified QR e-Challan generation.
* **AI Ore Blending Slider:** Dynamic formula adjusting Stockpile A & B mixing ratios to predict composite grade.
* **Fleet Fuel Anomaly Chart:** Visual bar chart highlighting Dumper-07's sudden diesel theft spike (+43.9%).

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

1. Open terminal in this folder:
   ```bash
   python -m http.server 8000
   ```
2. Open your browser and go to:
   ```
   http://localhost:8000
   ```
